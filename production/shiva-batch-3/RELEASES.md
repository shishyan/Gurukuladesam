Each new film has twelve distinct generated 16:9 scenes with gentle camera motion and the complete original audio. The Guru Kula Desam emblem stays at lower left. Paired dheepam and four slowly burning agarbatti on each side frame the lower corners, with rising white-grey smoke. Drizzle is confined to each outdoor ninth scene; restrained lightning is used for two storm scenes.
