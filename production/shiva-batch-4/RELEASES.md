# Lord Shiva songs — batch 4

Five distinct recordings selected from the Shiva catalog after checking prior production manifests and YouTube Studio film titles. A proposed Maasil Veenaiyum film was excluded because Studio already showed a film for that song. All five selected source uploads passed a 30-second-interval static-cover check. Each new film uses twelve distinct generated widescreen scenes with gentle motion, full original audio, the Guru Kula Desam emblem at lower left, paired dheepam and four slowly burning agarbaththi on each side with white-grey smoke. Drizzle appears only in outdoor scene nine; restrained lightning appears in the two storm stories.
