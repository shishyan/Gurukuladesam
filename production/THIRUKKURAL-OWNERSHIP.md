# Thirukkural song-film ownership and duplicate prevention

The complete pending source list and local files are in `publication-queue.json` and
`PENDING-VIDEOS.md`. Check both `thirukkural-batch-*` and `thirukkural-backlog-*` before
selecting a song. A completed local film counts as an existing video even while YouTube
publication is blocked. Avoid selecting another recording of an already filmed chapter.

The backlog films are finished, including original audio and the bottom-left logo.
Six parallel productions were discovered during the final cross-agent audit. Their
alternate backlog renders remain saved as backups but are explicitly suppressed from
upload in the central queue and the affected `ready.json` files.
