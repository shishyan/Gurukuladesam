## Locked visual continuity

- Arc: three seas and Tamil land → fertile Cauvery delta → temple civilization,
  literature, music, dance and learning → dignified Tamil Thai at sunrise.
- Palette: midnight indigo/bronze → monsoon teal/earth → Chola gold/crimson →
  saffron/ivory.
- Recurring motif: one steady oil flame and restrained natural jasmine where
  compositionally appropriate. Dhoopam is not mandatory for this film.
- Tamil geography, agriculture, architecture, instruments and materials must be
  culturally specific. No generic North Indian palace imagery.
- No flags, borders, party imagery or other political symbols. No text or
  watermark within narrative plates.
- Stable slow mechanical camera treatment only; no handheld shake, digital zoom,
  speed ramps or reframing pulses.
- Rain appears only in monsoon/agricultural contexts. Interiors remain dry.
