# ImageGen artwork briefs

Common brief: devotional South Indian Ganesha film in a coherent Tamil stone
temple and its grounds. Cinematic 35 mm texture, restrained warm practical
lamps and dawn light, physically plausible scale, no lettering or watermarks.
Images were generated as individual plates for slow camera movement. No image
is represented as captured live footage.
