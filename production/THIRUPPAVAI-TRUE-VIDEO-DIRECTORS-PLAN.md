## Story and visual arc

The film begins with Srivilliputhur still asleep under Margazhi moonlight. Andal hears the first temple bell, prepares the vow, and personally gathers eight companions. Their path grows from intimate thresholds to a confident nine-woman procession. At the temple tank, individual gestures become a shared Paavai vow. A reflection-driven, non-literal Narayana vision gives the prayer an inner culmination. The group then carries its offering into the temple as dawn turns the palette from indigo and silver through lavender and rose gold to dry, warm temple gold.

Weather is narrative, not wallpaper. Outdoor rain is finest at the beginning, becomes a soft drizzle at the tank, and ends before the mandapa. Wetness persists on stone, hems and hair after the rain stops. Interiors remain dry. Dhoopam appears only where a burner can physically exist—protected veranda, tank shrine, temple threshold and ritual tray—and its smoke is lit, occluded and deflected by the same environment.

## Locked continuity bible

- **Andal:** the same young adult Tamil woman throughout; maroon silk sari with emerald border, jasmine-wrapped long braid, white-and-red Vaishnava namam, simple antique temple-gold earrings, necklace and bangles. No crown, fantasy styling or costume changes.
- **Companions:** exactly eight recurring young adult Tamil women, each with one locked face, build, braid and sari: mustard, indigo, peacock teal, dusty rose, ivory-gold, burnt orange, muted violet and deep blue-green. Andal plus companions always equals nine when the full group is present.
- **Hero props:** one protected brass oil lamp, one tulasi-and-jasmine garland, one shallow brass flower tray, and one small perforated brass dhoopam burner. Their design, scale and patina do not change.
- **Geography:** the temple remains screen right during the approach. The group travels left-to-right until it reaches the sanctum; reverse angles may look back only after a clear eyeline or doorway cut.
- **Architecture:** period-neutral Tamil tiled houses, granite temple tank, pillared mandapa and authentic Srivilliputhur/Vaishnava forms. No North Indian palace language, modern wiring, vehicles, plastic, printed signage or electric practicals.
- **Light:** predawn indigo/silver with protected amber lamps; lavender and rose-gold dawn at the tank; warm, controlled lamp gold inside. Skin remains natural and exposure does not pulse.
- **Performance:** restrained devotional intention. No staged lip-sync, direct-to-camera singing, fashion poses, exaggerated smiles or repeated namaste tableaux.

## Rhythm and transition rules

- The planned size rhythm is deliberately varied: establishing wides establish geography, mediums carry human decisions, and details prove ritual tactility. Never replace two adjacent shots with the same framing and same move.
- Prefer straight cuts on completed actions: knock/open, lift/carry, footfall, pillar wipe, lamp entering shelter, hands settling, rise/depart and threshold crossing.
- Use only three restrained dissolves: S03→S04 flame match, S24→S25 prayer-to-ripple, and S29→S30 vision-to-awakening. Dissolves must bridge continuous motion, not hide broken generations.
- Opening and end typography is an edit-layer overlay on true-motion footage. Do not generate text inside footage and do not freeze the underlying frame.
- Each source should include at least 12 clean handles at head and tail (0.5 s at 24 fps). Editorial uses only the unique 8–10 second body specified above.

## Rain, wetness and dhoopam physics

- Outdoor rain shares a stable gravity vector, wind direction, drop scale and exposure within a scene. It occludes actors correctly, strikes skin/cloth/stone, creates localized splashes and accumulates believable wetness.
- Eaves, shrine roofs and interiors block rainfall. Water may drip at an edge or track from damp feet, but rain cannot pass through a roof. The mandapa and sanctum are dry.
- When rain eases, surfaces do not instantly dry. Preserve darkened hems, beads in jasmine/hair, puddles, runoff and wet reflections through S33; indoors, wet footprints may fade naturally near the entrance.
- Dhoopam belongs to a named physical burner. Smoke emerges from its apertures, is partly occluded by people/props, catches local light, diffuses with height and bends away from wind or foot traffic. It cannot remain screen-fixed, pass through objects, burn uncovered in heavy rain, or repeat an identical loop.

## Production and rejection gates

Reject the entire generated take on any single failure below; do not repair a failed shot with a still-frame motion effect.

1. **Not true motion:** frozen faces/backgrounds, Ken Burns movement, planar parallax, pulsing crop, looping particles, camera wobble, elastic architecture or a digital zoom posing as a dolly.
2. **Continuity:** wrong face/costume/prop, changed age or namam, group total other than nine when all are scripted, any person appearing/disappearing, direction reversal without coverage, or changing temple geography.
3. **Human anatomy/action:** extra or fused limbs, malformed hands/feet, foot sliding, broken gait, impossible object transfer, intersecting bodies, jewelry or jasmine crawling, or speech-like mouth motion.
4. **Weather physics:** rain indoors or through a roof, rain with no wet response, mismatched wind, splashes detached from footfalls, instantly dry cloth/stone, smoke unaffected by rain/air, or flame stable in exposed rain.
5. **Ritual/cultural accuracy:** modern objects, incorrect temple type, generic palace imagery, political symbols, spectacle deity, disrespectful handling of offerings, footwear in ritual areas, or incorrect Vaishnava marks.
6. **Image integrity:** face drift, temporal shimmer, texture boiling, duplicate people, background warping, exposure/focus pumping, crushed faces, text, logo, watermark, embedded captions or black/corrupt frames.
7. **Editorial fitness:** no clean handles, action begins before the shot or fails to resolve, two unrelated camera moves, unusable eyeline, unmotivated transition, or a composition that cannot accept the opening/end overlay.
