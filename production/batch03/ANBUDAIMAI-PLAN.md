## Film proposition

**A small act of care becomes reciprocal care across one family and its neighbours.** Show people noticing, making room, sharing effort and responding—not a procession of smiling strangers or a benefactor distributing gifts. The person first helped should contribute later, preserving agency and mutuality. No generic shrine/lingam/incense montage imported from Maasil.

Setting: one well-kept Tamil home courtyard and adjoining shared lane in contemporary traditional dress. Warm daylight, complete usable buildings, modest dignified interiors. Three anchor adults: grandmother in muted ochre sari, daughter in teal sari, neighbour in cream shirt/veshti; secondary family members only after continuity references exist. No caste/religious identity assumptions from clothes. Child optional, not essential to the story or casting.
