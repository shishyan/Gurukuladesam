# ImageGen artwork prompts

All frames were requested as cinematic 16:9, photorealistic 35 mm images for
the Tamil **Vaan Sirappu** monsoon film, with no text, logos or fantasy. The
specific subjects and physical constraints were:

| Asset | Prompt subject and constraints |
|---|---|
| VS40 | Dawn Tamil paddy valley; foreground irrigation channel, distant rain curtain, modest maintained temple; deep atmospheric layers. Brief establishing use because tiny farmers are static. |
| VS41 | Empty rain-fed paddy from low camera; rooted rice seedlings, shallow rippling water, earthen bund, distant palms and mountains; no people. |
| VS42 | Dry covered South Indian temple veranda; sheltered brass lamp, rain outside striking a temple tank, eave drips and gopuram; no people. |
| VS43 | Aerial Western Ghats foothills, layered monsoon clouds, forested valley and connected downhill river; no buildings or people. |
| VS44 | Close granite forest stream, water flowing downhill over wet rocks, anchored ferns and moss, active rain; no people. |
| VS45 | Banana grove in rain, attached leaves and trunks, water reaching ground channel, physically coherent wind and soil. |
| VS46 | Simple stone irrigation sluice, upper canal through one gate to lower rice channel, traceable downhill water. |
| VS47 | Village tank overflowing a stable stone weir into a lower canal, paddy beyond, natural ripples. |
| VS48 | Rooted rice stalks and water moving in soil at plant level, background paddy rows, no graphic or floating roots. |
| VS49 | Seasonal passage to mature golden paddy, attached panicles, retreating storm cloud and warm light; no people. |
| VS50 | Harvested rice grain in shallow baskets and tied sheaves under intact dry tiled veranda, rain beyond threshold. |
| VS51 | Covered cooking hearth, rice pot steaming from its own source, prepared banana-leaf communal table, rainy courtyard beyond; no people. |
| VS52 | Fixed stone spout pouring rain-fed water into one supported brass vessel, continuous water path, South Indian courtyard. |
| VS53 HOLD | Maintained temple passage with dry covered flooring and rain outside. Excluded: generated floor looks wet under roof. |
| VS54 | Brass dhoopam burner resting on dry ledge, thin smoke visibly connected to embers, rain far outside covered threshold. |
| VS55 | Plain Shiva lingam on proper yoni pedestal within maintained covered sanctum, simple flowers and safe lamps, rain only beyond doorway. |
| VS56 HOLD | Rice, banana leaf, brass vessel and flowers as covered offering with rainy tank beyond. Excluded: shelter surface looks wet. |
| VS56b | Tighter table-height offering of cooked rice, water vessel, jasmine and protected lamp; no floor visible, rain soft in distant doorway. |
| VS57 | Rural Tamil village on higher ground, connected irrigation and tank amid green paddy, intact houses, mountains and lifted clouds. |
| VS58 | High river-valley view, healthy downstream water around wooded islands toward agricultural plains, rain over distant hills. |
| VS59 | Dusk temple tank, natural rain impacts, anchored lotus leaves, intact lamp-lit temple and soft reflections. |
| VS60 | Closing living watershed, connected mountains, river, village tank, paddy and modest temple at believable scale in blue-gold dawn. |

These are image prompts, not footage prompts. The renderer adds camera and
environment motion; it does not create independent movement for people,
foliage, water depicted within a still, or ritual objects.
