## Visual grammar

- Preserve cream walls, terracotta roofs, soft green foliage, muted teal/ochre/earth clothing and natural skin. Gentle overcast exterior contrast; warmer shelter, not orange/teal blockbuster grading. Existing selected imagery is the look reference, not a new glamorous redesign.
- Care must have an observable choice and consequence: someone notices, makes room, assists, then receives participation or acknowledgement. Avoid endless seated smiles and anonymous generosity poses.
- Progress from intimate household care to cooperative work, mutual support and communal belonging. Alternate wide geography, medium action, selective detail and reaction. Do not repeat the same frontal veranda composition throughout.
- Camera movement must reveal a relationship or space. Prefer held composition, short lateral drift or restrained pullback; not constant zooming. End an action before cutting away; do not cut every ten seconds merely because a generator delivers ten seconds.
- Rain is a physical environment: coherent wind direction, falling streaks at differing depths, contact ripples/splashes outdoors, roof runoff and wet ground. Roof masks/occlusion protect sheltered heads, clothing, table and floor away from the eave. Some edge dampness is plausible; blanket rain over an interior is not.
- Dhoopam only in a motivated domestic/devotional moment, emitted from a visible burner. It is not universal fog. Anbudaimai does not require a temple insert; do not import Maasil shrines or smoke to satisfy an effects checklist.

## Hybrid pipeline — use the technology for the job

1. **Imagegen:** create reference-locked scenic keyframes, production-design alternatives, character/wardrobe references and clean extension/background plates. Use existing selected frames as visual references where possible; read imagegen skill before generating. Wide frame margins must permit motivated movement without cutting limbs/architecture. Generated writing prohibited. Reference images alone are NOT finished moving coverage.
2. **Generated video:** animate those keyframes where the available normal interface supports image conditioning. Otherwise use the image as a look reference and explicitly flag identity continuity unproven. Generate one manageable action and independent environmental motion per shot. Human interaction, cloth, water and foliage must change physically, not only via camera push. Test one continuity pair before spending a whole batch.
3. **Compositing:** foreground/background separation, tracked occlusion masks, restrained depth-appropriate rain, surface impacts, reflections and subtle relighting may finish already credible footage. A scene that is sunny/dry or has frozen people cannot be repaired with a uniform rain overlay. Masks must follow moving heads/hands/eaves; inspect edges through time. Preserve provenance marks; do not inpaint them away.
4. **Editing:** keep continuous original `source/youtube/lneosghJWgs.m4a`; discard generator audio. Build unique-source range ledger and whole-film coverage map. No loops, repeated prefixes, freeze extension, speed manipulation or recycled Maasil footage to fill the duration. New image-based shots may count only after genuine temporal animation and review.
5. **Finish:** coherent exposure/palette across each sequence, natural rather than excessive sharpening, measured transitions. Upscaling may be considered only after native-frame QC and must not be called native 4K detail. Verified editorial typography only; no generated gibberish.
